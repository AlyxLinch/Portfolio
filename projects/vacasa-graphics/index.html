Overview

Local materials, national brand voice.

I created cohesive branded materials for flyers, office signs, welcome packages, and fridge info magnets used across eight offices. The work had to feel local and useful without drifting away from Vacasa’s brand system.

Challenge + solution

Consistency across offices.

I used Vacasa’s Brand Center to study visual standards, voice, and asset usage, then built each piece around clarity: guests needed the right information quickly, and office teams needed materials they could trust.
